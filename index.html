Research

Quantum-Secured Federated Learning for Healthcare

Delhi Technological University · 2025

Hospitals want to train shared models without sharing patient data. Federated learning allows that — but its key exchange will not survive a quantum adversary. This work closes that gap.

  • A hybrid quantum–classical protocol protecting federated learning across medical IoT devices from both present and quantum-era threats.
  • A hybrid QKD system combining E91 and BB84, built in Qiskit, detecting eavesdroppers through QBER analysis.
  • A classical layer of AES-GCM, digital signatures and SHA-256, with defenses against gradient-inversion attacks.

Projects

Quantum Key Distribution & Eavesdropping

Qiskit · Python · Arduino

  • Simulated the BB84 protocol end to end in Qiskit — Alice preparing photons in randomly chosen bases, Bob measuring in his own, and the two sifting a shared key from the rounds where their choices agreed.
  • Introduced an active eavesdropper who must measure before forwarding, and traced how that measurement collapses the state and corrupts the key she is trying to steal.
  • Measured the resulting rise in quantum bit error rate, the threshold above which a key is discarded — security here rests on physics, not on a computational assumption.
  • Built a physical demonstration with an Arduino Uno, servo-driven polarising film and lasers, so the basis choice and its consequences can be watched on a bench rather than read off a plot.

Superdense Coding Simulator

Qiskit · Python

  • Built a scalable simulator covering all four Bell states, with the encoding, transmission and decoding stages separated so each can be inspected independently.
  • Recovers two classical bits from a single transmitted qubit, the sender applying one of four operations to her half of an entangled pair before sending it on.
  • Verified correct decoding across repeated multi-run trials, confirming the protocol holds rather than succeeding by chance on a single shot.
  • Makes the underlying point concrete: entanglement is a communication resource, and prior shared correlation doubles what one qubit can carry.

QAOA as Adiabatic Evolution

Planck'd 2025, IIIT Bangalore · Third place

  • Modelled QAOA as Trotterized adiabatic evolution, proving the circuit reproduces the adiabatic path exactly in the limit of infinite layer depth.
  • Built a warm-start parameter-transfer protocol that carries optimal angles learned on three-node graphs across to four-node systems, cutting the optimiser's search substantially.
  • Established that freely varied parameters outperform fixed linear schedules, with the QAOA cost expectation bounded below by the adiabatic one.
  • Placed third at Planck'd 2025, IIIT Bangalore, with the accompanying technical report.

Quantum-Assisted Physics-Informed Neural Networks

BQP Challenge, WISER Quantum + AI 2026 · PennyLane · JAX · PyTorch

  • Replaced the first hidden layer of a physics-informed neural network with a variational quantum circuit, then benchmarked the hybrid against a classical baseline on Burgers', heat and Navier–Stokes equations.
  • Designed the experimental framework and evaluated six circuit configurations — varying qubit count, data encoding and entangling ansatz — holding parameter counts comparable so the comparison stayed fair.
  • Found a sharp drop in gradient variance moving from three to four qubits, consistent across all three equations: expressivity and trainability pull against each other.
  • The five-qubit hybrid gave a statistically significant accuracy gain on Navier–Stokes, though the wider conclusion is that quantum layers help in specific cases, not universally.
